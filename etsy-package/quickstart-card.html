Face Quest

In-browser face recognition lab • webcam + optional BBC micro:bit V2
No install • No account • 100% local • English printables
1
🌐

Open the App

In the project folder run python3 -m http.server 8012, then open http://localhost:8012 in Chrome or Edge. Allow the webcam.

2
🚀

Start Mission

Click 🚀 Start Mission. Three tiny AI models load from ./models: face detector, landmarks, recognition. The scanner turns on.

3
🧠

Teach My Face

Center your face and click 🧠 Teach My Face. The app collects sharp samples and saves a 128-number descriptor locally — no photos.

4
🔓

Unlock!

Click 🔓 Unlock!. The app compares live frames to your saved face. Match → ✅. No match → ❌. Tweak Threshold for strictness.

5
🔗

Pair the Robot Buddy (optional)

Flash the MakeCode BLE UART sketch to your BBC micro:bit V2. Click 🤝 Connect, pick BBC micro:bit [xxxxx]. On Unlock, the micro:bit shows ✓ / ✗, drives P0 HIGH on MATCH, and acks over UART.
