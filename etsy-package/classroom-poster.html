Teach the App
Your Face!

Face Quest • Classroom Guide

1
📷

Allow the Camera

Open index.html in Chrome. Click 🚀 Start Mission. When the browser asks, allow the webcam. Your face appears in the scanner window.

2
🧠

Teach My Face (Enroll)

Center your face, click 🧠 Teach My Face. Three little AI brains turn your face into secret numbers — no photos saved. Done in seconds.

3
🔓

Unlock! (Verify)

Step back, step forward. Click 🔓 Unlock!. The app compares live numbers to your saved face. Match → ✅. No match → ❌.

4
🔗

Robot Buddy (optional)

Flash the MakeCode BLE UART program to a BBC micro:bit V2. Click 🤝 Connect, pick your board. On Unlock, the micro:bit shows ✅ / ❌ and can play sounds.

5
⭐

Earn XP, Discuss Privacy

Each Teach + Unlock earns XP in the top bar. Nothing leaves the computer — no cloud, no accounts. Perfect jump-off to talk about how real face recognition works.
