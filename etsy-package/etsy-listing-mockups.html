Three Tiny AI Brains

Face Quest uses three small face-api.js models, all running locally in your browser.
STEP 1
👤

Face Detector

Scans each frame and finds where a face is. Pick SSD for accuracy or Tiny for speed.
STEP 2
📍

Landmarks

Marks 68 points — eyes, nose, mouth — so the face can be aligned before recognition.
STEP 3
🧬

Recognition

Turns the aligned face into a 128-number descriptor. Compare two descriptors → match!

Your Face, Your Laptop

Every model runs locally. Every descriptor lives in your browser’s IndexedDB. Click Reset Face at any time to wipe it.
📷

No Photos

The webcam stream is analysed live — nothing is recorded to disk.

📴

No Internet

Models load from ./models. After first load, Face Quest works offline as a PWA.

🔑

No Account

No login, no cloud, no analytics, no telemetry. Ever.
